NARA SCOUT

A Reconnaissance Tool for FRUS Compilers

Declassified Documents · Withdrawal Sheets · Unprocessed Series

Purpose

Scout the Bush 41 and Clinton presidential records at NARA for three FRUS-compiler workflows:

  1. Harvest declassified documents for possible inclusion in FRUS volumes.
  2. Inventory withdrawal sheets to identify classified material still requiring MDR/FOIA.
  3. Map unprocessed collections that lack descriptive data, for research planning.
